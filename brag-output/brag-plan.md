# Brag Plan: LeadPulse Harvester Pro

## What is this app?
LeadPulse Harvester Pro is an autonomous AI Google Maps B2B lead generation engine with zero browser automation, AI hierarchical geo-planning, multi-threaded email enrichment, and live SSE streaming to an Alexandria editorial dashboard.

## The angle
Developers and growth agencies waste thousands of dollars on expensive Google Maps APIs or struggle with brittle, sluggish Puppeteer/Selenium scrapers that get bot-blocked within minutes. LeadPulse bypasses the browser entirely using direct internal Protobuf (`tbm=map`) reverse-engineering, generating 100+ verified phone numbers and business emails in seconds at zero API cost.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Giant bold serif typography slams onto the screen:  
**"Stop paying $0.05 per Google Maps lead."**  
Followed by a fast flash of: **"Zero Headless Browsers. Raw Protobuf Speed."**

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **The AI Geo-Split & Prompt:** The query *"Dentists in Miami with verified email"* is entered; AI instantly fractures the metro into micro-locations with coordinate bounding.
2. **The Protobuf Data Flood:** Live counter explodes from 0 to 100 leads in real-time; place_id deduplication token skips duplicates on the fly.
3. **The Enriched Lead Card:** Business card snaps in showing Business Name, Verified Email, Local Phone, Rating, and Website Status (Has Real Website).
4. **1-Click Export & Dual Storage:** Instant CSV/XLSX export button glowing, backed by SQLite WAL local buffer + Supabase cloud sync.

## Outro / punchline
**"Own your pipeline. 100% autonomous."**  
LeadPulse Harvester Pro logo and Alexandria v2.4 badge settle into a clean, confident dark backdrop.

## User flow worth showing
Entry: Natural Language prompt input typing out target city and criteria.  
Key action: Engine starts — terminal streams raw Protobuf coordinates and SSE live updates while harvested counter ticks rapidly.  
Result: Complete leads table populates with verified contact info, ready for instant export.
